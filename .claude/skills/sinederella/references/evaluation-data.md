## Tool comparisons recorded

### Search engines (`docs/MASKING.md:17-34`, planted 8 Mb genome, 120 bp family)

| Tool | Finding |
|---|---|
| ssearch36 36.3.8g, stock, `-S` or not, lowercase mask | mask ignored: 1,500 DOM + 20 SUB found; 37-46 s/query |
| ssearch36, DOM rewritten to N | 0 DOM, 20 SUB, 20 RARE; ~4 s |
| ssearch36 rebuilt `-DDNALIB_LC -S` | masks whole library sequences, loses the 20 unmasked SUB copies: wrong tool for genome windows |
| blastn 2.17.1 word 11 | 20/20 at 100 % identity, 4/20 at 85 %, 0/20 at <= 75 % |
| blastn-short | 20/20 at 85 %, 17-20/20 down to 45 % |
| ssearch36 | 20/20 at every identity tested |
| minimap2 `-k 11 -w 5` | nothing for any family ("repeat k-mers exceed `mid_occ`, and case is folded") |

Real genome (`docs/MASKING.md:121-132`): masking VES (6 % of tbr) changed
Rhin-1 69 -> 51 s, others +-10 %; 70 % of Rhin-1 "hits" sat on VES copies.

### Other comparisons

- `sear` vs `sear_multi`: `benchmark_sear.sh` (3 genomes truncated to 100 Mb,
  5 queries, wall time + BED identity, `:15-20,279-280`); no committed result.
- nhmmer rescue of unplaced old copies: 9 of 145 rescued, 0 of 109 MEG-T2
  (`flankscan/HANDOFF.md:190-193`), not implemented.
- COSEG vs SubFam route: design fixed before runs in
  `docs/COSEG_COMPARISON_DESIGN.md`; results above. COSEG itself is forward-only
  (`COMPARISON_LOG.md:88`).
- SubFam awk consensus vs EMBOSS `cons`: 3,600 runs identical; `kmer_order.c`
  vs numpy ordering byte-identical (`COMPARISON_LOG.md:34-38`).
- MAFFT modes: threaded L-INS-i 3-7x faster but different alignment, worse on
  hard plates (`step8a:53-57`, `tests/bench`).
- Satellite kind-B k-mer pre-screen: rejected, 31 of 625 pairs at >= 70 %
  identity share < 1 % 16-mers (`docs/SATELLITES.md:338-344`).
